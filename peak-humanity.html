The brief

An NGO renovating schools and building kitchens through education and sports. When I joined as the first team member, it didn't even have a name.

What I did

  • Built the social strategy from zero and ran Instagram, Facebook, X and TikTok, plus the web copy
  • Ran the full social campaign for its biggest fundraiser in 2025, managed creator partnerships and wrote a brand ambassador playbook so supporters could carry it further
  • After Typhoon Tino hit Palawan in November 2025, coordinated an on-the-ground response: food and bed-building supplies for 30+ families

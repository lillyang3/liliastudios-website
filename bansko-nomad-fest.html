Bansko Nomad Fest

Bansko Nomad Fest · Bansko, Bulgaria · Social media, content, UGC

Trend-led Reels for a 500K+ view festival

The brief

The world's largest digital nomad festival. Hundreds of remote workers, creators and founders in one mountain town, all with their phones out. The content had to move faster than they did.

What I did

  • Owned strategy and production for trendy short-form video: ideas, filming, editing, scheduling
  • Trend-reactive edits and candid BTS, posted while the moment was still warm
  • Built for the algorithm, without losing the raw, in-the-room feel
  • Made the Reel that won the festival's Reel Fest

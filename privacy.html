Privacy

Privacy policy

Short and plain: what happens to your data on liliastudios.com. Last updated 16 November 2026.

Who is responsible

This website is run by Lilia Angelicheva, trading as Lilia Studios, through Liviya Fashion VT Ltd, a company registered in Bulgaria. Liviya Fashion VT Ltd is the controller of personal data processed through this site. For anything about your data, write to hello@liliastudios.com.

When you visit the site

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. To deliver the pages and protect them from attacks, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you request and the time of the request. This is necessary to run a secure website (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not set its own cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. Fonts and images are served from this site itself, not from third parties.

Instagram posts on project pages

Some project pages show posts embedded from Instagram, a service of Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. When such a page loads, your browser connects to Instagram, which receives your IP address and may set its own cookies. If you are logged in to Instagram, Meta may link the visit to your account. Meta is responsible for that processing; see Instagram's privacy policy.

Booking a call and emailing me

Calls are booked through TidyCal, which processes the details you enter there under its own privacy policy. Messages sent through the contact form are delivered to my inbox by Web3Forms. If you book a call or email me, I use your name, contact details and message to reply, to prepare a proposal and, if we work together, to run the project and invoice it (steps before a contract and the contract itself, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). I keep enquiries only as long as needed to answer them, and project and invoicing records for as long as Bulgarian accounting and tax law requires. I never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Data outside the EU

Cloudflare, Meta and TidyCal may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where they do, they rely on safeguards recognised under the GDPR, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive a copy of it. Email hello@liliastudios.com and you'll get an answer within one month. You can also complain to Bulgaria's Commission for Personal Data Protection (cpdp.bg) or the data protection authority where you live.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

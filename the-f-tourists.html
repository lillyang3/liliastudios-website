The F Tourists

The F Tourists · Lisbon, Portugal · Strategy, social media, UGC

From 4.9K to 157K views a month

The brief

Two social hostels in Lisbon, We Love F Tourists and We Hate F Tourists. Loud name but quiet Instagram. Polished, static, could've been any hostel anywhere. They wanted their Instagram to feel as human as the place itself. Not a party hostel, because they're not one: they're all about great vibes, real community and spotless rooms.

The catch

The full name is The F*cking Tourists. Try putting that in every caption and see how long Instagram stays friendly. The attitude had to come through without the swear word doing all the work.

What I did

  • Swapped staged room shots for actual humans: guests, staff, real moments
  • Local tips, on brand: don't be just a f*cking tourist
  • Trend-led Reels, me on camera included
  • Kept the clean rooms in frame, so "social" never read as "messy"

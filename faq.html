Frequently asked questions

What do you actually do?

Four things under one roof: brand strategy, social media and content, partnerships, and events. Some brands need one of them, most end up needing a mix. I build the plan, then I make it happen.

Who do you work with?

Hostels and colivings, festivals and events, NGOs and purpose-led brands. Basically any brand that runs on people. If you bring people together, we'll get along.

How does working together start?

Two ways to find out if we're a match: book a free discovery call, or send me a message with more info and context about your brand and idea. If we click, I send you a tailored proposal with a clear scope, timeline and price.

How much does it cost?

There are no fixed packages, because no two brands need the same thing. The price depends on the scope, the deliverables and how many of the four services you need. The fastest way to a number is a free discovery call, or a message with some context about your brand and idea.

Do you only do strategy, or also the content?

Both. Strategy that never leaves the deck is just a nice PDF. I plan the content, then shoot, edit, write and post it, from Reels and carousels to on-camera UGC, captions and web copy.

Do you always shoot everything yourself?

No. If you already have someone on site who can film, that's perfectly fine, and if you have raw footage, we can work with that too. I'll shape it into content that fits the plan.

What is an AI brand brain?

One organised home for everything that makes your brand yours: your voice, your audience, your content pillars, your do's and don'ts. It's set up so the AI tools you use write and plan like your brand, not like everyone else's.

How do you use AI?

To make the work faster and smarter, not to replace the craft. I set up AI brand brains, automate a big part of the content process like planning, captions and repurposing, and use AI to speed up research and editing. You get more done, and more of the time goes into the creative work that matters. And I always check everything myself: final decisions are made by me, and nothing sees daylight without my approval.

Why do I need you if I can ask Claude to generate all of my social media designs?

That's great, use it! My mission is to avoid AI slop and create stylish designs, while automating as much of the process as possible with AI. Human taste and judgement are still what make content work. I don't compete with AI. I use it to my best advantage, and to yours.

Where are you based? Do you work remotely?

I'm based in Bulgaria and work remotely with brands worldwide. So far that's brands in 9 countries. For shoots, events and launches, I come on site when the project needs it.

Do you work alone?

I lead every project myself and bring in my crew of trusted creatives when it needs more hands. You always have one point of contact: me.

Which languages do you work in?

English and Bulgarian. I also know some German, Japanese and Dutch, enough to create content in them, though not enough to speak them fluently.

How does payment work?

Through my company in Bulgaria. Payment is usually made at the start of the work; larger projects can be split into two halves. VAT is added where it applies.

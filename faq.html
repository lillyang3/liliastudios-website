FAQ

Questions, answered.

How working with me on brand strategy, social media, partnerships and events actually works.

What do you actually do?

Four things under one roof: brand strategy, social media and content, partnerships, and events. Some brands need one of them, most end up needing a mix. I build the plan, then I make it happen.

Who do you work with?

Hostels and colivings, festivals and events, NGOs and purpose-led brands. Basically any brand that runs on people. If you bring people together, we'll get along.

How does working together start?

With a free 15-minute call. We talk about where your brand is now and where you want it to go. If we click, I send you a tailored proposal with a clear scope, timeline and price.

How much does it cost?

There are no fixed packages, because no two brands need the same thing. The price depends on the scope, the deliverables and how many of the four services you need. The fastest way to a number is a free 15-minute call.

Do you only do strategy, or also the content?

Both. Strategy that never leaves the deck is just a nice PDF. I plan the content, then shoot, edit, write and post it, from Reels and carousels to on-camera UGC, captions and web copy.

What is an AI brand brain?

One organised home for everything that makes your brand yours: your voice, your audience, your content pillars, your do's and don'ts. It's set up so the AI tools you use write and plan like your brand, not like everyone else's.

Where are you based? Do you work remotely?

I'm based in Bulgaria and work remotely with brands worldwide. So far that's brands in 8 countries. For shoots, events and launches, I come on site when the project needs it.

Do you work alone?

I lead every project myself and bring in my crew of trusted creatives when it needs more hands. You always have one point of contact: me.

Which languages do you work in?

English and Bulgarian. I also know some German, Japanese and Dutch, enough to create content in them, though not enough to speak them fluently.

How do you invoice?

Through my company in Bulgaria, so working together is a straightforward business-to-business setup.

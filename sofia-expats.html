Sofia Expats

Sofia Expats · Sofia, Bulgaria · Events, partnerships, visual content support

Being the bridge between expats and Bulgaria that I wished I'd had abroad

The brief

A weekly newsletter and platform for English-friendly events in Sofia. My job was to find the right partners and build events with them that people actually leave the house for.

What I did

  • Ran paid media partnerships for the newsletter, from first email to signed deal
  • Togeda × Sofia Expats: a friendworking event for 100+ expats with Togeda, a Bulgarian app for organising gatherings and activities matched to people's interests. I ran the partnership end to end and hosted on the night.
  • Rakia Museum × Sofia Expats: premium rakia tastings for expats. I ran the collab start to finish, worked on the design, and made the promo video and posters. Taste-testing was a mandatory part of the job... (Try the melon rakia at the Rakia Museum!!)

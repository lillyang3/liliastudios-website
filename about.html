About

Hi, I'm Lilia. With a Я.

In Bulgarian my name is Лилия, and it ends in Я. That's the backwards R in the logo. I find it a super unique letter, and I see it as part of my identity.

1 / 4

Photography: Jacob Medler Photography (2026)

Bulgarian-born, Creative Business graduate from the Netherlands (2025), with a semester in Japan studying Japanese business, culture and language. Since then I've worked with brands in 8 countries: Bulgaria, the Netherlands, the UK, Switzerland, Germany, Portugal, Tanzania and the Philippines.

Working across cultures taught me that what lands in Lisbon can fall flat in Sofia. So instead of copy-pasting playbooks, I take time to get to know each audience first, then build the plan around them.

I do strategy, social media, partnerships and events because to me they're one job. Strategy that never leaves the deck is just a nice PDF.
